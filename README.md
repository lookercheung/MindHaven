# MindHaven Privacy Policy (English)

**Effective Date: September 26, 2026**

MindHaven (the "App") is built on one principle: **your data belongs only to you.** This policy explains how the App handles — in fact, does not handle — your information.

## 1. We Collect Nothing

1. The App has **no servers, no accounts, and no analytics**.
2. Your chats, AI companion settings, photos, videos, favorites, and purchase status are stored only on your device.
3. The App never transmits your content to any third party. Nothing is sold, shared, or synced.

## 2. How Data Is Stored

1. All data lives in the App's sandbox, accessible only to the App.
2. The database is excluded from iCloud backups; your content never leaves for the cloud.
3. Uninstalling the App permanently and irreversibly deletes all data. No copy exists in any cloud or with the developer.

## 3. About Network Access

The App **needs no network** to function. The only system-level network touchpoints are unrelated to the App itself:

1. In some countries/regions, the system keyboard (soft keyboard) service may connect on its own — controlled by the OS and keyboard provider, not by the App.
2. Subscriptions and restore purchases are handled by Apple's App Store; the App never touches your payment information.

## 4. Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Photo Library | Sending/saving photos & videos, choosing avatars |
| Microphone | Push-to-talk voice input |
| Speech Recognition | On-device speech-to-text |
| Face ID / Touch ID | App lock verification |

Declining any of these does not affect offline chatting.

## 5. Minors

1. **The App requires users to be at least 13 years old (or the higher minimum age required by local law).**
2. Minors should use the App with the knowledge and guidance of a parent or guardian.
3. Because the App collects no data (including age), we cannot and need not verify age; the first-launch age confirmation is an honest self-declaration.

## 6. Your Rights

Since all data stays on your device and the developer has no access to it, you have complete control by design: view, export (via system share), or delete (in-app, or simply uninstall).

## 7. Changes

This policy may be revised with app updates; revisions will be published on this page.

# MindHaven 隐私政策（Privacy Policy）

**生效日期：2026 年 9 月 26 日**

MindHaven（以下简称"本应用"）的设计原则是：**你的数据只属于你**。本政策说明本应用如何处理（实际上是不处理）你的信息。

## 一、我们不收集任何数据

1. 本应用**没有服务器、没有账号、没有analytics（分析统计）**；
2. 你的聊天记录、AI 伙伴设定、照片、视频、收藏、订阅状态等，全部只保存在你的设备本地；
3. 本应用不向任何第三方传输你的内容，不存在数据出售、共享或跨设备同步。

## 二、数据如何存储

1. 所有数据保存在本应用沙盒目录中，仅本应用可访问；
2. 数据库已标记为排除 iCloud 备份，你的内容不会被上传到 iCloud；
3. 卸载本应用 = 彻底、不可逆地删除全部数据，云端与开发者处均无任何副本。

## 三、网络使用说明

本应用正常运行**不需要网络**。仅以下情形会涉及系统级网络访问，且均与本应用本身无关：

1. 某些国家与地区的系统输入法（软键盘）服务可能自行联网（由系统与输入法提供商决定，本应用无法控制）；
2. 订阅与恢复购买由 Apple 的 App Store 系统处理，本应用不接触你的支付信息。

## 四、权限使用

本应用可能请求以下系统权限，用途如下，拒绝不影响离线聊天功能：

| 权限 | 用途 |
|---|---|
| 照片库 | 发送与保存聊天图片、视频，选择头像 |
| 麦克风 | 按住说话的语音输入 |
| 语音识别 | 语音转文字（在本机完成） |
| 面容 ID / 触控 ID | App 密码锁验证 |

## 五、未成年人保护

1. **本应用要求用户年满 13 周岁（或所在国家/地区法律规定的更高最低年龄）**；
2. 未成年人应在监护人知情与指导下使用；
3. 由于本应用不收集任何数据（包括年龄），我们无法也无需验证年龄，首次启动的年龄确认是基于诚实的自我声明。

## 六、你的权利

因为所有数据都在你的设备本地、开发者无法接触，你天然拥有对数据的完全控制权：查看、导出（通过系统分享）、删除（应用内删除或直接卸载）。

## 七、政策变更

本政策可能随应用更新修订，修订后将在本页面公布。
